02 Projects

Academic capstone

Health AI: Multi-Agent Healthcare Triage

Problem
Community health workers need decision support for symptom assessment and triage.
Solution
Five sequential Google ADK agents with four function tools for hybrid RAG, drug-safety lookup, vital-sign extraction and severity scoring. Structured JSON outputs, plus a separate deterministic safety evaluator.
Outcome
On 50 fixed cases, accuracy rose from 48% (keyword-rule baseline) to 84%. Emergency recall reached 89% and under-triage fell from 46% to 6%.

Transparency note: the safety gate was not met, the system is not clinically validated, and human review is required. I report this deliberately.
